- Only 31 minutes of data collected vs. 24 hours planned
- PSI never breached threshold (100m) during the 31 minutes
- HPA continuously scaled down rather than up

### Root Cause
- PSI generated by stress test was insufficient to exceed 100m threshold
- Workload may be too efficient (only 160m PSI observed)
- Stress pods may not be generating enough memory pressure
